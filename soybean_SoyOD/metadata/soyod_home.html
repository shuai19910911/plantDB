About


Module

Genome

A total 59 de novo assemblies of soybean genomes: 53 chromosomal and 6 telomere-to-telomere (T2T) level

Phenome

Comprehensive phenotypes of 225 agronomic traits and ~2500 images of cultivated and wild soybean accessions

Population

The 984 and 3904 datasets, including population structure and selection sweeps

Transcriptome

RNA-seq data from 162 new libraries and 935 published libraries from different tissues and conditions

Variome

SNPs and InDels from 984 new, high-depth re-sequencing and integrated total ~4K re-sequencing accessions

Synteny

Chromosomal and genomic collinearity of 50 chromosomal level genomes and 5 T2T genomes
